Need help with something tinyrack makes? Start here.

## Get help

- **Community forum:** [forum.tinyrack.net](https://forum.tinyrack.net/) for questions, builds and troubleshooting.
- **GitHub:** [github.com/tinyrack-net](https://github.com/tinyrack-net) to file an issue on the repository for the app you use.
- **Email:** contact@tinyrack.net

## Apps

### TinyTrayManager

CPU, memory and network readouts in the Windows system tray.

- **The readouts are hidden.** Windows puts new tray icons in the overflow area (`^`). Turn TinyTrayManager on under **Settings > Personalisation > Taskbar > Other system tray icons**, or drag the icons out of the overflow.
- **Start with Windows.** Turn on **Run at sign-in** in the tray menu, or use **Settings > Apps > Startup**.
- **Settings and logs.** `%APPDATA%\tray-monitor\config.toml` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\tray-monitor\log.txt`. The Microsoft Store build keeps both in the package's `LocalState` folder.
- **Source and issues:** [github.com/tinyrack-net/tray-monitor](https://github.com/tinyrack-net/tray-monitor).

### Other apps

- [Dotweave](https://dotweave.tinyrack.net) syncs configuration across machines with git.
- [Proxer](https://proxer.tinyrack.net) puts a private HTTP service behind a public URL you control.
- [Tinyauth](https://tinyauth.tinyrack.net) adds authentication to your own services.

When you report a bug, include your OS version and the relevant log lines.
