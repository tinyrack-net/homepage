tinyrack is a small homelab workshop. This policy covers tinyrack.net, the community forum, and the open-source desktop apps we publish.

## This website

tinyrack.net is a static site. It loads Google Tag Manager, which loads Google Analytics. Those tools may set cookies or read device identifiers to measure visits. Google processes that data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not sell or share it.

## The community forum

[forum.tinyrack.net](https://forum.tinyrack.net/) is a separate service. It stores the account and content you create there and has its own terms.

## Desktop apps

Our desktop apps (TinyTrayManager, Dotweave, Proxer, Tinyauth and others) run on your PC:

- They do not collect, transmit or sell personal data, usage data or telemetry.
- Settings and logs stay on your machine. TinyTrayManager writes `%APPDATA%\tray-monitor` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\tray-monitor`, or the package's `LocalState` folder for the Microsoft Store build. Other apps document their own paths.
- Apps installed from the Microsoft Store are processed by Microsoft under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). That is separate from the app itself.

## Your choices

You can block analytics with your browser's tracking protection or an extension. You can delete local app data by removing the app's folders. For requests about data on the forum, contact us.

Questions or requests: contact@tinyrack.net, or [open an issue](https://github.com/tinyrack-net).
